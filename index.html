Shift briefing

  • Keep the machines producing. Every running machine adds output.
  • Faster line speed means more output and hotter machines.
  • Above 95 °C for 5 seconds, a machine breaks down.
  • Running hot wears a machine down. At 0% health it breaks.
  • To repair: switch the machine OFF, then hold Repair for 3 seconds.
  • Use the Forecast lines and the What-if test to act before problems happen.
  • Level up early: each mission upgrades your factory. Everything produced after it counts extra: ×1.1 after mission 1, ×1.2 after 2, ×1.35 after 3, ×1.5 after all 4.
  • Score = output + level bonus + 150 per mission − 200 per breakdown.
